Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Who we are

Craft Jump is provided by Tecnometro. This policy applies to the updated Craft Jump app for iPhone and iPad. For privacy questions or requests, contact tecnometro.correo@gmail.com.

Your game progress

Scores, gems, unlocked characters, completed challenges, selected worlds and sound preferences are stored locally on your device. Craft Jump does not require an account and does not operate its own gameplay server or its own analytics service. Device backups managed by Apple may contain local app data, depending on your settings.

Advertising and consent

Craft Jump uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded videos. Completing a rewarded video grants in-game gems. The Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages advertising privacy choices where applicable. We request updated consent information when the app starts and request ads only when the SDK permits it. Where required, an Ad privacy options control lets you revisit your choices.

Data processed by advertising services

Google advertising and consent services may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from that address, device or app identifiers, advertising interactions, app interactions, crash information, performance measurements and other diagnostic data. These services use data to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, provide consent functionality and maintain or improve their services. Data handling depends on your region, consent choices, device settings and the services in use. The current app does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization. This does not mean that advertising services process no data.

How information is used and shared

Tecnometro uses locally stored progress to operate the game and information you voluntarily send by email to answer support or privacy requests. Advertising data is processed by Google and its advertising service providers under their applicable terms and privacy policies. We do not operate a separate database of players or sell your local game progress. Advertising disclosures or transfers may be treated as sharing or targeted advertising under laws that apply in some regions; use the privacy choices offered to you.

Your choices and rights

You can play without creating an account. Rewarded videos are optional. Use Ad privacy options when it is displayed to manage applicable advertising consent, and use your device privacy settings for additional controls. Removing the app removes its local app data from that device; backup copies may remain under your Apple backup settings. We cannot remotely access or reset your locally stored gems or scores. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information, withdraw consent, object to certain processing, or complain to your local data protection authority. Email us for requests concerning information you sent directly to Tecnometro. For data held by Google, use Google privacy controls or contact Google.

Legal grounds and international processing

Where applicable, processing may rely on your consent, providing the game or answering your requests, or legitimate interests such as security and fraud prevention. Advertising providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their privacy terms and applicable safeguards. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that lawfully took place before withdrawal.

Retention and security

Local progress remains on your device until it is removed or overwritten. We retain support messages only as needed to respond, resolve the matter and meet applicable obligations. Google determines retention of data it processes under its policies and controls. We take reasonable measures to protect information we handle, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Audience

Craft Jump is intended for teenagers and a general audience and is not designed specifically for young children. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to Tecnometro, contact us so we can address it. Local age and consent requirements may differ.

External links and this website

Advertisements and external links may take you to services with their own privacy practices. This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and access logs, to deliver and secure the site. Tecnometro has not added analytics scripts, advertising or contact forms to this website.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. The updated date identifies the latest version. Privacy and support contact: Tecnometro — tecnometro.correo@gmail.com.
